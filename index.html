About

Professional Summary

Dr. Muhammad Ahsan is an internationally trained researcher specializing in SHM, data analysis (vibration, acoustic, and SCADA), nonlinear signal processing, and AI-driven diagnostics. He earned a PhD in engineering and technical science, and disciple of Automation, Electronics, Electrical Engineering and Space Technologies from the Silesian University of Technology (SUT), Poland (2025), focusing on low-cost MEMS-based vehicle engine diagnostics, advanced digital signal processing, and AI classification models—resulting in peer-reviewed journal papers and conference publications. He also holds a Master’s degree in Control Theory and Engineering from Nanjing University of Science and Technology, China and Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering from the Air University, Pakistan.

Dr. Ahsan’s core competences include advanced time–frequency analysis (STFT, CWT, spectral kurtosis, EMD), AI architecture design (hybrid CWT–DCNN–LSTM, LeNet5–LSTM), and development of experimental platforms for low-cost sensing in rotating machinery, engines, and wind turbines. Proficient in Python and MATLAB, he combines theoretical innovation with hands-on implementation. His teaching and mentoring at SUT, along with professional training in optimization, reinforcement learning, structural dynamics, and project management, reflect a strong commitment to knowledge transfer and research leadership.

Dr. Ahsan has authored over 20 publications in reputed journals and conferences, contributing to the advancement of fault diagnosis in bearings, machine health monitoring, and control system design. His research aims to improve the reliability, safety, and efficiency of industrial systems, with a particular emphasis on optimization, nonlinear control, and robust multidisciplinary approaches. Through his work, Dr. Ahsan continues to push the boundaries of knowledge, making significant contributions to both academia and industry.
